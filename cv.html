Research interests

Blacklisting and sanctions. Proscription of terrorist organisations. Counter-terrorism and political exclusion. Transgovernmental policy networks and policy transfer. Digital security and cyber security governance. Global governance.

Policy engagement

International organisations

  • Advises the United Nations on blacklisting and sanctions in East Africa and Afghanistan.
  • Research cited at the International Court of Justice in The Hague.
  • Consults on blacklisting and sanctions compliance for UNDP in Afghanistan, the Swiss Refugee Council and World Vision.

Australian government

  • Works with the Department of Defence and the Department of Home Affairs on protecting liberal democracy from digital threats.
  • Research has supported training for the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade, Home Affairs, Defence, the Australian Federal Police and the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet.
  • Appointed expert advisor to the Commonwealth Inspector of Transport Security on aviation and maritime security in 2016.

Parliaments and reviews

  • Work has formed the basis of submissions to a Queensland Parliamentary committee, the COAG Review of Counter-Terrorism Legislation and the Commonwealth Independent National Security Legislation Monitor.

Training officials

  • Has delivered public policy training to federal officials in Canberra and to government officials from Bhutan, Indonesia, Mexico, South Korea and Vietnam.
